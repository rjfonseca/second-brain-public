# Structurizr

[Structurizr](https://structurizr.com/) is a diagram DSL for creating C4 model diagrams.

The problem this with this DSL is that it's controlled by a company and the tools are bound to their paid plans.

They have a [cli](https://github.com/structurizr/cli) but it's useless. It's just for pull and pushing diagrams form their platform.

This blog post has some interesting ideas:

[Auto-generated C4 Architecture Diagrams in Go](https://threedots.tech/post/auto-generated-c4-architecture-diagrams-in-go/)
